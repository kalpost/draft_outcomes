Why "If He Hits" instead of raw Median (p50)? NFL rookie-contract receiver outcomes are bimodal: prospects either earn a starting role (~1,800–3,800+ yds) or get derailed by injuries/depth-chart burial (<600 yds). An unconditional p50 blends both regimes into a misleading ~1,200-yard middle ground that few players actually finish at. Each prospect is shown with their 80% Outcome Range (p10–p90), their "If He Hits" Starter Projection (green tick), and their "If He Misses" Downside. Actuals are displayed for completed classes through (white dot = landed inside p10–p90, red = outside).
