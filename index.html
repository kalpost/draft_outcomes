Why "If He Hits" + 80% Range instead of raw Median (p50)? NFL rookie-contract receiver outcomes are bimodal: prospects either earn a starting role (~1,800–3,800+ yds) or get derailed by injuries/depth-chart burial (<600 yds). Each prospect's range track spans their 10th-percentile Floor (p10) to 90th-percentile Ceiling (p90), with the blue marker indicating their expected 3-year total If He Hits as an NFL starter. For completed classes through , the circular dot shows actual 3-year NFL receiving yards (green = inside p10–p90, red = outside).
